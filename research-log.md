## Promotion rules

Every candidate is assigned one of four homes:

| Home | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Build catalog | Small, reproducible project that teaches a primitive |
| Landscape | Existing implementation worth studying or using |
| Hall of Fame | Exceptional shipped artifact or build story |
| Watchlist | Interesting but too new, too opaque, weakly documented, or risky to endorse |

We do not promote projects solely because they have many stars, use a new model, or claim “zero lines written.” The collection values inspectability, a useful result, a documented method, and a lesson that survives the model release cycle.

## Next research passes

- **AI-native interfaces:** voice, multimodal canvas, spatial UI, and agents that manipulate rich documents.
- **Data and simulation:** synthetic data engines, game worlds, digital twins, and test environments.
- **Model internals:** small models, inference runtimes, quantization, tool-call training, and verification.
- **Maintenance:** projects that use agents for dependency upgrades, incident response, accessibility, and long-term refactoring.
- **Geography and language:** strong projects from India, Southeast Asia, Africa, Latin America, and non-English communities.
- **Negative evidence:** abandoned agents, unsafe wrappers, broken demos, misleading benchmarks, and projects whose claims do not survive inspection.
