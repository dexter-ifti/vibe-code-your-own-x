# People & Feeds

The best projects often surface before they have a polished landing page. This page keeps a small, high-signal set of people, engineering publications, and social posts that help us discover them.

Social links are discovery signals, not endorsements or canonical documentation. When a post points to a project, paper, course, or technique, we promote the underlying primary source only after checking that it is real, accessible, and useful to builders.

## Feed-to-catalog workflow

1. **Discover:** capture the original post, author, date, and any linked artifact.
2. **Verify:** open the repository, paper, documentation, or course itself; record the canonical URL.
3. **Classify:** place it in the build catalog, landscape, Hall of Fame, learning paths, or watchlist.
4. **Extract:** write the smallest buildable primitive it teaches and link it to a catalog brief when possible.
5. **Date:** record when the source was last checked; stale or deleted links should be marked rather than silently removed.

The goal is not to turn this into an influencer directory. A feed earns a place when it consistently leads to inspectable work, primary sources, or a transferable engineering lesson.
